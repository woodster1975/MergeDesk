# MergeDesk logo

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, then encoded as a Windows ICO with 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 pixel representations. The transparent PNG is the original refined generated artwork. App colours and the folded-envelope motif connect the mark to personalised mail.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a single polished original symbol for MergeDesk, a friendly professional Windows mail merge desktop application. Symbol only, no words or letters. A crisp geometric folded envelope formed by two converging paper ribbons suggesting individual messages coming together. Flat vector-like silhouette, bold simple shapes readable at 32 pixels, balanced square composition with modest padding. Established app palette: evergreen #193F3E and teal #146C60, with a warm pale mint accent #DDECE5. Use pale mint and fresh teal visible on both a dark evergreen header and a light desktop. Transparent background, no canvas tile, no shadow, no gradient, no mockup, no tiny decorative details, no Microsoft or Outlook logos.

## Refinement prompt

Refine this MergeDesk logo for shipping as a tiny Windows application icon. Preserve exactly the overall folded envelope/ribbon shape and composition. Replace every gradient and textured area with perfectly uniform solid flat colors: dark evergreen #193F3E, teal #146C60, pale mint #DDECE5. Remove the dark blotch/noise on the left panel entirely. Clean smooth crisp antialiased edges. No extra marks, no text, no shadow, no new elements. Keep true transparent background and generous small square padding. Flat vector-like graphic.
