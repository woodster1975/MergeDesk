MergeDesk - Windows x64

Extract the entire ZIP, then double-click MergeDesk.App.exe.
The .NET desktop runtime is included; no SDK installation is required.

Need instructions? Click Help (F1), or press F1. Search the guide or
choose a topic. Hover over any option for a tooltip. Watch walkthrough
video opens the included 4:26 captioned MP4; pause and replay as needed.
Keep HelpAssets with the application. USER-GUIDE.md is the written guide.

New workflow improvements:
- Tick recipients in Recipients; search only hides rows, it does not exclude them.
- Use Exclude all, then search and Include shown to choose a small group.
- Results offers Send test email: one selected row, only to your test address,
  with CC and BCC cleared and the subject marked [TEST]. Confirm before sending.
- Your workspace autosaves after idle edits. Restore autosave or Start fresh
  appears at startup. Recovery never reconnects Outlook or resumes a send.
- Reloading changed source data unticks every recipient until reviewed.

In Compose, write your email in the visual Email body editor.
Select text and use the font, size, bold, italic, underline, colour,
alignment and list controls. The app generates HTML automatically.
Use Insert merge field to add personal details from recipient data.
The Subject box has its own field dropdown and Insert merge field in subject button.
Use Insert image in the body editor for embedded PNG/JPEG/GIF images (up to 2 MiB).
Save your project to keep the pictures and template together.
Advanced HTML source is optional and collapsed by default.

Load samples/recipients.csv or samples/recipients.xlsx to try a dry run.
To prepare messages in Outlook:
1. Open Outlook connection and choose classic Outlook or Microsoft 365/Outlook.com.
2. Connect to a configured classic Outlook account, or follow the README's
   one-time Microsoft app-registration setup and sign in through your browser.
3. Validate and preview, then click Create Outlook drafts in Results.
4. Open the selected account's Drafts folder in Outlook, review each email,
   and click Send when ready.

The Microsoft connection works with both new and classic Outlook for
Microsoft-hosted mailboxes.

To send directly from MergeDesk:
1. Replace the generic example text and signature with your actual message.
2. Validate and preview using addresses you control for the first test.
3. Connect classic Outlook, or enable direct sending and sign in to Microsoft.
   The Microsoft app registration needs delegated Mail.Send permission.
4. In Results, choose Send emails and check the final confirmation.
5. Check Outlook Outbox/Sent Items and received mail.

Sending is paced and logged. Repeat direct-send runs skip identical messages
already submitted by this feature. Interrupted/uncertain sends require review;
there is no automatic retry that risks sending another copy. Earlier drafts
sent manually in Outlook are not included in this send record.
Review previous results before repeating a draft batch; a new batch creates
new drafts. Real Outlook/Microsoft connections need testing on your account.
See README.md for setup, limits and build instructions.

New templates and pictures:
- Compose: expand Templates & signatures to save or apply reusable messages.
- New signature opens its own visual editor; Append signature adds it at the end.
- Picture settings: choose an embedded picture, set width and alternative text.
- Alignment moves the whole paragraph; use a separate picture line.
- New instructions are in Help and USER-GUIDE.md; the video covers the earlier workflow.


New: Results has Clear history, with confirmation. It clears all saved runs and displayed results while keeping exports, local run logs, Outlook messages and duplicate-send protection. MergeDesk now has its own logo and Windows icon.
